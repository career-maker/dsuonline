Why DSU Online
MBA?

The Online Master of Business Administration (MBA) at Dayananda Sagar University Online MBA, also known as dsu online mba and dayananda sagar university online mba, is designed for future business leaders and ambitious learners seeking career growth through one of the best online MBA programs in India and among the best online MBA colleges, making it an ideal online MBA for working professionals across industries. This UGC-entitled online MBA degree offers a flexible 2-year curriculum that blends strategic thinking with real-world business skills, catering to learners pursuing an online MBA in Bangalore as well as students across online MBA India. As a leading online MBA university, DSU provides best-in-class specialisations including online MBA marketing, MBA finance online course, online MBA in business analytics, online MBA HR, and MBA supply chain management online, ensuring industry relevance. With affordable online MBA programs, live expert sessions, and career-focused learning, the Dayananda Sagar University Online MBA empowers learners to build confidence, leadership capability, and business expertise needed to succeed in today’s competitive global business environment.

Eligibility Overview

A candidate who has passed in a Bachelor’s Degree of minimum three years duration recognized by this University or who has passed any other examination recognized by this University as equivalent there to is eligible to apply.
The candidate shall have secured not less than 50% aggregate marks in all years of the qualifying degree examination. Candidates belonging to SC / ST / OBC categories are eligible for a relaxation up to 45%, as per applicable government norms.

Admission Process
A Step-by-Step Guide

Step 1: Register Yourself

Create your account on the admission portal to begin your journey.

Step 2: Verify Email ID & Phone Number

Confirm your email and mobile number through OTP for secure access.

Step 3: Pay Application Fee

Provide your personal, academic, and professional information accurately.

About Financial Managament

The Financial Management elective builds advanced expertise in financial markets, investment strategies, corporate restructuring, and global finance. Learners gain strong analytical capabilities to make data-driven financial decisions.

Learning Modules

Financial Accounting & Reporting

Understand financial statements, accounting principles, balance sheets, income statements, and cash flow analysis.

Corporate Finance

Learn capital structure, cost of capital, financial decision-making, and maximizing shareholder value.

Investment Analysis & Portfolio Management

Analyze investment opportunities, risk-return trade-offs, portfolio diversification, and asset valuation.

Financial Planning & Budgeting

Develop budgeting techniques, forecasting models, financial controls, and performance measurement systems.

Risk Management & Financial Strategy

Manage financial risks, hedging strategies, regulatory compliance, and long-term financial sustainability.
